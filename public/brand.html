ELEVEN11 · BRAND01 — THE IDEA
The line

Make a wish. We’ll build it.

What we are

An AI solutions studio. Every solution we make is a device — sound, image, motion, events, stories — and the devices plug into each other to make the result.

ELEVEN11 · BRAND02 — THE MARK, IN ONE BREATH
The form · the wish

Loose, pixelated, still growing. It changes size and shape with each use; it is never the same twice.

The squares · the build

Exact, on one grid: a solid square and an outline square, sharing an edge, one step apart.

The colon · the minute

The only thing that moves, and the only thing in orange. It blinks like a clock.

ELEVEN11 · BRAND03 — THE GRID
The cell

One cell is half an icon square. A 16:9 page is 32 × 18 cells; the form, the masses and the icon all sit on it.

The mass

Every page is a mass drawn in whole cells. Two sides break — top and bottom, one cell in or out. The sides stay straight. No straight cut between pages, ever.

Eleven loose pieces

Each mass sheds exactly 11 pieces: pixels on the ground, holes in the mass.

The calm core

Text and pictures live in the core. The grid never breaks there.

One loud block

One orange thing per page: the colon, or whatever is live.

Layers

The ground grid moves slower than you. The edges break into pixels as they leave the centre of the screen, and the loose pieces fly faster. That is the parallax.

ELEVEN11 · BRAND04 — FOUR RULES AND A VOICE

No slants, ever. Every edge is horizontal or vertical.

Every part is a working device: it does something when you touch it.

Square 34 · step 8 · gap 3. Every size comes from the icon.

Orange means now: the colon, what is live, what to press. Nowhere else.

The voice · like a device talking

Short, warm, exact. Say what happens, not how clever it is.

ELEVEN11 · BRAND06 — MOTION
